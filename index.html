Our Heritage & Passion

Generous Flavours, Authentic Middle Eastern Soul

At Open Sesame Sydney, we believe a meal is never just food — it is an invitation to gather, share, and experience true Levantine warmth.

Nestled at Sydney Place on George Street, our kitchen honors timeless Middle Eastern culinary traditions. Every dish begins with authentic techniques: fragrant whole spices toasted in-house, chickpeas slow-simmered into velvety hummus with pure sesame tahini, meats marinated for 24 hours, and skewers seared to smokey perfection over live coals.

Whether you are dropping in for a swift CBD lunch, enjoying a lavish family feast, or picking up dinner on your commute home, our generous plates and warm hospitality will make you feel instantly at home.
